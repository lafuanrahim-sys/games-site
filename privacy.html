Raalhu Rush privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. Published by Aranzo Investments, Hulhumale, Maldives.

Raalhu Rush does not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads.

What the game stores

Your island progress, stars, lives, boosters and settings are saved on your device only. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads

Raalhu Rush shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to get extra moves or a life. These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is covered by the Google partner sites policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change this choice later in the game's settings. On any Android phone you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings, under Google, then Ads.

What we collect

We, the developer, do not collect or store any personal information. The game has no account, no chat and no analytics of its own, and there are no in-app purchases.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to load ads and vibration for haptic feedback. You can turn haptics off in Settings.

Children

Raalhu Rush is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
